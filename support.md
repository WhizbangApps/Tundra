  <h1>Support</h1>
  <p class="updated">We read every message.</p>

  <p>Email <a href="mailto:hello@whizbangapps.dev">hello@whizbangapps.dev</a> and we'll get back to you. The quickest way is Settings → Say Hi! in the app, which fills in your device, iOS version and app version for you.</p>

  <h2>Common questions</h2>

  <h3>Where is my data stored?</h3>
  <p>Only on your device. Tundra has no account and no server, and your episodes are never uploaded. Because of that, deleting the app deletes your history, and it does not move to a new phone on its own unless you restore a device backup.</p>

  <h3>How do I log an episode?</h3>
  <p>Tap “I lost it” on the Home tab. Tundra records the time, walks you through a short breathing exercise, then lets you add the intensity, trigger and a note. You can skip the log and just breathe if you prefer.</p>

  <h3>How do I delete an episode?</h3>
  <p>Open the History tab and swipe the episode to the left.</p>

  <h3>The widget shows the wrong number</h3>
  <p>The widget counts calendar days since your last episode and refreshes at midnight. If it looks stale, open Tundra once and it will refresh straight away. If it still looks wrong, email us with your device and iOS version.</p>

  <h3>How do I remove the ads?</h3>
  <p>Open Settings (the gear on the Stats tab) and tap Remove Ads. It is a one-time purchase with nothing to cancel.</p>

  <h3>How do I restore my purchase?</h3>
  <p>Open Settings → Remove Ads and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.</p>
